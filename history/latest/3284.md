- **[jdbc-v2]** `ResultSet#getObject(int, Class)` on the result set returned by `Array#getResultSet()` can now read
  elements of `Date`/`Date32`, `DateTime`/`DateTime64` and `Time`/`Time64` arrays as `LocalDate`, `LocalDateTime`
  and `LocalTime`. The values are taken from the elements as read from the server, without a conversion through
  `java.sql.Date`/`Time`/`Timestamp`, so they do not depend on the JVM time zone.
  (https://github.com/ClickHouse/clickhouse-java/issues/3284)
