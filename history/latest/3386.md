- **[jdbc-v2]** `setObject(..., java.sql.Date)` now works on the RowBinary writer
  (`beta.row_binary_for_simple_insert`) for `Date` and `Date32`, including dates nested in
  `Array`, `Map`, `Tuple` and `Nullable`. Previously it failed with
  `IllegalArgumentException: Cannot convert <date> to Long`.
  (https://github.com/ClickHouse/clickhouse-java/issues/3386)
