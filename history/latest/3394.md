- **[jdbc-v2]** The RowBinary writer (`beta.row_binary_for_simple_insert`) now stores
  `DateTime`/`DateTime64` values for columns without an explicit time zone in the **server**
  time zone, matching the text path and the reader, instead of UTC. Behaviour change on
  non-UTC servers for the beta writer path; pin the zone with `use_time_zone` /
  `use_server_time_zone` to keep the old behaviour.
  (https://github.com/ClickHouse/clickhouse-java/issues/3394)
