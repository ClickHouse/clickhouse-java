- **[jdbc-v2]** `Connection#commit`, `rollback`, `getCatalog`, `getNetworkTimeout` and both `setClientInfo` overloads
  now throw an `SQLException` (`SQLClientInfoException` for `setClientInfo`) with SQLState `08000` connection
  exception "Connection is closed" after the connection was closed, as the JDBC contract requires. Before, they returned a
  value or silently did nothing, and with `jdbc_ignore_unsupported_values=true` `commit`/`rollback` did not report the
  closed connection either. `close()`, `isClosed()` and `isValid()` are unchanged.
  (https://github.com/ClickHouse/clickhouse-java/issues/3270)
