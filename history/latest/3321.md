- **[client-v2]** Hardened native LZ4 and ZSTD block compression against truncated or malformed streams. Transfer
  failures now consistently surface as `DataTransferException`, codec size mismatches are rejected, and the maximum
  compressed or uncompressed block allocation is configurable with `compression.max_block_size`
  (`Client.Builder#setMaxCompressionBlockSize`). The allowed range is 1 MiB through the 1 GiB protocol
  maximum, and the default is 64 MiB.
  **Breaking change:** compressed read failures that previously surfaced as `IOException`, `EOFException`, or a
  wrapping `ClientException` now surface as the unchecked `DataTransferException`. Applications that catch one of
  the previous exception types must catch `DataTransferException` instead and restart a failed streamed query from
  the beginning.
  JDBC now also recognizes socket timeouts nested under compressed-read exceptions and applies its network-timeout
  connection handling.
  (https://github.com/ClickHouse/clickhouse-java/issues/3321)
